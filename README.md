# Sajid Kabir Saji

Aeronautical engineer (BEng, 2022). I build open engineering tools for flight:
simulation, atmosphere and weather models, and mission planning, with a
research focus on autonomous decision-making and solar-electric endurance
for UAVs.

## Projects

- **[solar-uav-sim](https://github.com/sajidkabir/solar-uav-sim)** -
  Solar-powered UAV endurance simulator: 24-hour energy-balance modeling
  with a charge-consumption parity solver. The open-source companion to my
  undergraduate thesis on a solar-powered quadcopter. v1.0.0 released.

In progress, shipping one at a time:

- ISA atmosphere and aerodynamics utilities
- METAR/TAF aviation weather decoder
- NACA airfoil generator and analyzer
- Multirotor mission planner

## Focus

- Onboard autonomous decision-making for drones
- Solar-electric flight endurance and energy management
- Open tools that students and researchers can actually run
